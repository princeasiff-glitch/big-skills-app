Our Simple Promise

Big Skills for Little Ones does not collect, store, share, or transmit any personal information — from children or adults. This app was built for families, with privacy as its foundation. There are no accounts, no logins, and no tracking.

Privacy Policy

Information We Collect

None. We do not collect any personal data of any kind, including but not limited to: names, email addresses, phone numbers, location, photos, contacts, device identifiers, usage analytics, or advertising data.

Any information a child types into the app — such as their name on the completion certificate — stays only on your device. It is never sent to us or to anyone else, and we cannot see it.

Children's Privacy

This app is designed for children and is built to comply with children's privacy standards, including the U.S. Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA), the EU/UK General Data Protection Regulation for children (GDPR-K), and India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act (DPDP).

Because we collect no personal information whatsoever, there is nothing to misuse, sell, or expose. We do not knowingly or unknowingly gather data from children, because we do not gather data from anyone.

No Ads, No Third Parties

This app contains no advertisements and no third-party tracking or analytics tools. Your child will never see ads, pop-ups, or content from outside companies.

Read-Aloud Feature

The app includes an optional "read aloud" feature (a speaker button) that uses your device's own built-in voice to read stories aloud. This feature:

  • Does not use the microphone
  • Does not record any sound
  • Does not send any data anywhere

It simply uses your device to speak the on-screen text. No listening, no recording, no data.

Works Offline

The app is designed to work fully offline. It does not require an internet connection to use, and it does not send or receive data over the internet during normal use.

Purchases

If the app offers a paid unlock or purchase, that transaction is handled entirely by the app store (Apple App Store, Google Play, or the platform you downloaded from) under their payment systems and privacy policies. AFSR never receives or stores your payment details.

Links Outside the App

Any links that lead outside the app (for example, to a website or store page) are placed behind a parental gate so that only an adult can access them. We are not responsible for the privacy practices of external websites.

Changes to This Policy

If we ever update this policy, we will post the new version here with a revised "Last updated" date. Any future change will continue to honor our core promise: your family's privacy comes first.

Frequently Asked Questions

Does the app collect any information about my child?

No. The app collects no personal information at all — no names, no location, no photos, no usage data. Anything typed in (like the certificate name) stays only on your device.

Is it safe for my child to use alone?

The app is safe and free of ads, tracking, and outside links (any links are behind a parental gate). That said, it's designed to be enjoyed together — the stories, activities, and "talk about it" prompts are richest when a grown-up shares them with the child.

Does the app read the stories aloud?

Yes — there's an optional speaker button on each skill. Tap it and the story is read aloud using your device's built-in voice, so pre-readers can enjoy it too. Prefer to read it yourself or have your child practise reading? Just leave the button off. It's completely optional.

Does the read-aloud feature listen to or record my child?

No — never. It only speaks the on-screen text using your device. It does not use the microphone and records nothing.

Are there any ads?

No. The app has no advertisements and no third-party content of any kind.

Does it need an internet connection?

No. The app works fully offline, so you can use it anywhere — at home, in the car, or on a plane.

What ages is it for?

It's designed for children ages 4–10. Younger children can enjoy the stories and read-aloud with a grown-up; older children can read along and try the activities themselves.

How many skills are included?

The app teaches 75 big life skills across 9 friendly sections — kindness, confidence, feelings, friendship, curiosity, good habits, responsibility, staying safe, and being wonderfully yourself. New skill packs may be added over time as free updates.

How is this different from the book?

The book is for reading together; the app adds read-aloud narration, tap-through activities, progress tracking (a growing skill garden), and a fill-in certificate — turning the lessons into an interactive experience your child can return to.

Do you offer refunds?

Purchases are handled by the app store you downloaded from (Apple, Google, etc.), so refunds follow their policies. Please contact the store directly for purchase questions.
