💬 Why "talk about it" matters

The lessons stick best when you chat about them. Each skill has a gentle question to ask your child — turning a story into a real conversation.

🔒 Privacy

This app collects no data — no names, no location, no tracking. Everything stays on this device. The read-aloud uses your device's own voice and never records anything.

🔤 Read-aloud

Tap the speaker on any skill to have the story read aloud — perfect for pre-readers. Leave it off for children practising reading. Completely optional.

🛡️ A note on the Safety section

Section 8 covers real safety skills — body safety, tricky people, online safety and emergencies. Please read these together with your child, at their pace.
